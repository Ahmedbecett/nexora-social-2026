## Full description (EN)
**Nexora** is a free social network for sharing photos, short videos (reels) and stories with your friends, with a clean, familiar dark and light design.

**Features**
- 📸 **Posts & carousels** – share photos and videos with captions, hashtags and @mentions.
- 🎬 **Reels** – full-screen vertical videos with likes, comments, sharing and saving, plus a *Friends* tab for reels from people you follow.
- 🌍 **Reels from around the world** – thousands of real, freely-licensed videos (Wikimedia Commons, with author and license credit) by topic: nature, animals, space, ocean, cities, sports, dance, music, food and travel.
- ⭕ **Stories & highlights** that disappear after 24 hours.
- 🔎 **Explore & search** – find any account by username or name, search reels and hashtags.
- 💬 **Direct messages**, notifications, follow requests and private accounts.
- 👤 **Profile** – notes, multiple links (WhatsApp, Facebook, websites), reposts and tagged posts.
- 🌗 **Dark / light mode** and **50+ languages** including Arabic, English, French, Kabyle and more.
- 🔐 Sign in with Google, email or username.

Nexora is free, contains no fake accounts or fake content, and respects your privacy. Privacy policy: https://nexora-social-2026.vercel.app/privacy.html
