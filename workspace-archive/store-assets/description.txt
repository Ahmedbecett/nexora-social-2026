Nexora is a free social network for sharing photos, short videos (reels) and stories with your friends, with a clean, modern design in dark and light mode.

Features:
- Posts and photo albums: share photos and videos with captions, hashtags and mentions.
- Reels: full-screen vertical videos with likes, comments, sharing and saving, plus a Friends tab for reels from people you follow.
- Reels from around the world: thousands of real, freely licensed videos from Wikimedia Commons, with author and license credit, organized by topic: nature, animals, space, ocean, cities, sports, dance, music, food and travel.
- Stories and highlights that disappear after 24 hours.
- Explore and search: find any account by username or name, and search reels and hashtags.
- Direct messages, notifications, follow requests and private accounts.
- Profile with notes, multiple links (WhatsApp, Facebook, websites), reposts and tagged posts.
- Dark and light mode, and more than 50 languages including Arabic, English and French.
- Sign in with Google, email or username.

Nexora is free, has no fake accounts or fake content, and respects your privacy.
