01

Scope

This policy covers the public pages of the BECETTI ecosystem on this site. It does not replace the privacy policy of a separate product. For example, NEXORA AI has its own privacy policy on its product site.

02

Information requested by this site

The public site does not offer account registration or a form that collects your details. It may make a public catalogue request to display products and their links; that request does not send your name, email address or sign-in token.

03

Technical logs and hosting

The hosting service and API may process technical request information needed to operate and protect the site, subject to their configurations and terms. We do not control the hosting provider’s logs or retention periods; consult the provider for details.

04

Email and external links

If you email us, your email provider processes your message and address. Links to stores, products and other services are governed by their operators’ policies. Do not send passwords, verification codes or secret keys by email.

05

Tracking and cookies

The public BECETTI pages do not add advertising, analytics or tracking cookies. Your browser or hosting provider may use technologies needed to deliver or protect the service.

06

Children and updates

This site is a public product directory, not a service designed to collect children’s information. We may update this policy when site functions change; the update date appears above.

07
