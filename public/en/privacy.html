1. Who we are and what this policy covers

NEXORA AI is a web platform for AI chat and app building (with an optional Android app), developed and operated by Ahmed Becetti (AI BECETTI), who is the controller of the personal data processed through the service.

This policy applies to nexora-social-2026.vercel.app, the Android app, the public information pages (About, Features, Download, FAQ), NEXORA Studio, and any request sent through the service.

It does not cover third-party services you open from links inside the platform — such as GitHub, Vercel, Google or ad networks. Each has its own privacy policy.

2. Data we collect

Account and identity

  • Name and username, email address or phone number, optional profile photo, and interface language.
  • Internal account identifier, sign-in method (email, Google or phone), account creation date and last sign-in date.
  • Passwords are stored as a one-way hash, never as readable text, and cannot be recovered by us.

Content you send

  • Messages, prompts and generated replies, conversations saved to your account, and images you ask us to generate.
  • Files you intentionally upload (PDF, DOCX, text, images) and the processing needed to read them.
  • NEXORA Studio project files, terminal commands, project revisions and restore points, and publish links when you publish.
  • Usage preferences stored in your account (preferred model, reply language, display settings).

Optional integrations

  • When you connect Google Docs/Drive, GitHub or Vercel we request only the scopes the feature needs, and store access tokens encrypted on the server.
  • No file from those services is read or sent unless you explicitly ask for it, and you can disconnect an integration at any time.

Subscriptions and payments

  • Subscription status and plan, transaction reference, and the payment identifier issued by the provider (PayPal, or a postal/bank transfer for paid plans).
  • We never store card numbers or banking verification codes; those are entered directly with the payment provider.

Technical data

  • IP address, browser, operating system and device type, requested page and time, and error messages — for security and troubleshooting.
  • The Android advertising identifier (Google AdMob) as configured on your device.

What we do not request or collect

  • No location, contacts, text messages, call logs, or photo-library access without your explicit choice.
  • Android permissions are limited to internet, network state and microphone — the last one only while you use voice input.

3. Why we use your data

  • Run the service: create your account, sign you in, verify by email or phone, and restore access.
  • Answer your requests: send the prompt to an AI provider, return the reply, and save the conversation to your account.
  • Build and publish: generate project files, run commands and publish an app only when you explicitly ask.
  • Security: prevent abuse, fraud and limit evasion, and detect attacks and outages.
  • Subscriptions: manage your plan, limits and receipts where applicable.
  • Service messages: verification codes, security alerts and material policy changes. We do not send marketing without consent you can withdraw.
  • Performance: aggregate, non-profiling measurements of speed and stability.

4. Legal bases for processing

Where the EU GDPR or a similar law applies, each purpose relies on a clear legal basis:

  • Performance of a contract: providing the service you asked for (account, conversations, Studio, publishing).
  • Legitimate interests: service security, abuse prevention and stability — balanced so your fundamental rights are not overridden.
  • Consent: personalised advertising, optional notifications and third-party integrations. You can withdraw consent at any time in settings.
  • Legal obligation: keeping records required by law or answering a valid legal request.

5. How AI requests are processed

When you send a request, its text — with only the context needed, such as a file excerpt or the attachment you selected — is sent to one or more providers to generate the answer: OpenAI · Google Gemini · OpenRouter · Groq · Mistral · DeepSeek · xAI · Cerebras · GitHub Models · Pollinations · llm7.io, depending on what is available and enabled in the current deployment.

  • We do not use your content to train models, do not sell it, and do not use it for ad targeting from our servers.
  • A provider may retain a request for a limited period under its own policy; we prefer providers that offer a no-retention option where available.
  • Do not send sensitive data (medical, financial, identity documents, passwords) in prompts. Answers can be wrong, and verifying them is your responsibility.
  • You can delete a conversation or project in the interface, which removes its content from your account.

6. Cookies and local storage

We use limited local storage in your browser to run the interface, and we do not use it to track you across other websites:

  • Essential: nx_theme (theme), nx_view (chosen view), and session or sign-in tokens — the platform cannot work without them.
  • Third party: ad networks (Google AdSense, AdMob, Monetag, Adsterra) may set cookies or identifiers for delivery, measurement and fraud prevention; this varies by country and your consent.
  • Global Privacy Control: we honour the browser's GPC signal; when it is set, the Adsterra and Monetag units are not loaded on web pages.
  • You can clear local storage or block third-party cookies in your browser settings; some features, such as staying signed in, may stop working.

7. Advertising and how the service is funded

On the website we may show Google AdSense, Monetag and Adsterra ads. Adsterra loads as a Native Banner and Monetag as OnClick/Popunder units on public pages, sign-in screens and chat; Monetag may open a new window or tab when clicked.

Ad networks may process technical data such as IP address, browser identifiers, cookies and page interaction to serve and measure ads. Third-party ad code runs inside the web page and may technically read its content and user input; we do not send conversation history from our servers to ad networks for targeting, but we cannot guarantee what third-party code reads inside your browser.

In the Android app we show Google AdMob only: a banner under the welcome screen, a rare interstitial (at most once every 10 minutes and three times a day) on a natural transition, and an optional rewarded ad the user requests. AdMob never appears during text or voice conversations or over dialogs. Consent is handled by the Google UMP framework where it applies, and you can accept or refuse it in the in-app consent dialog.

Managing personalised ads: Google Ads Settings · aboutads.info. Choosing non-personalised ads does not remove ads; it reduces personalisation.

8. Who we share data with

We do not sell your personal data. We share only the minimum necessary with processors working on our behalf:

  • Hosting and runtime: Vercel (functions and site delivery) and Vercel Blob (image and file storage).
  • Database: Neon (managed PostgreSQL) for accounts, conversations and projects.
  • Sign-in: Google Firebase Authentication for email, Google or phone verification.
  • Email and messaging: Brevo for service email, and an SMS gateway (sms-gate.app or Textbee) to deliver a verification code to your number; both receive only the number and the message text.
  • AI: the providers listed in section 5, to answer your request.
  • Advertising: the networks listed in section 7.
  • Publishing you request: GitHub and Vercel when publishing a project, and Google Docs/Drive when you connect them for reference material.
  • Payments: PayPal or a postal/bank transfer for paid plans; payment details are handled by the provider and not stored by us.
  • Legal compliance: we may disclose data if compelled by a valid legal request or to protect our rights and user safety.

9. How long we keep data

  • Account and content: until you delete the content or the account.
  • After account deletion: removed from the database immediately; encrypted backups may persist up to 30 days and are then destroyed.
  • Error and security logs: up to 90 days.
  • Subscription and billing records: for the period required by tax or accounting law.
  • Verification codes: a few minutes, then they expire.

10. Security

  • Encrypted HTTPS for all requests; provider keys stay on the server and are never shipped to the browser.
  • Hashed passwords, encrypted integration tokens, and production access limited to the operator.
  • No system is 100% secure; if you discover a vulnerability or suspect an incident, email us immediately using the address below so we can act.

11. Your rights

Depending on your country, you have the right to access your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to processing, port it, and withdraw consent at any time with no retroactive effect.

  • Most actions are available in account settings (edit your details, delete conversations and projects, delete the account).
  • For anything else, email us below. We answer within 30 days at most and may ask you to verify your identity to protect the account.
  • You may complain to your local supervisory authority — in Algeria, the National Authority for the Protection of Personal Data (ANPDP) — or to the data protection authority in your EU member state.

12. Deleting your account and data

  1. Open Account & Security → Danger zone → “Delete my account and all my data”.
  2. The account and its content (conversations, outputs, generated images, projects) are deleted permanently and cannot be restored.
  3. To remove only part of it, delete an individual conversation or project in the interface without deleting the whole account.
  4. We may retain limited records where the law requires it, or to prevent repeated abuse.

13. Children

The service is not directed to children under 13, or under the minimum age in your country (up to 16 in some EU states). We do not knowingly collect children's data; if we learn a child under that age holds an account, we delete it. If your child created an account, contact us at the address below.

14. Generated content and acceptable use

  • Outputs may be inaccurate, biased or incomplete; review them before publishing or making an important decision.
  • You are responsible for what you publish and for not infringing third-party rights.
  • Do not use the service to harm others, break the law, generate illegal content, evade usage limits or attempt to compromise the service.

15. International transfers

Our providers operate in several countries (EU and the United States), so your data may be processed outside your country of residence. We rely on providers offering recognised transfer safeguards — such as standard contractual clauses — where required, and we ask them to protect data to at least the standard described here.

16. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when features or providers change. The update date appears at the top of the page and in the page's structured data, and material changes are announced in the app or by email before they take effect. Continuing to use the service after an update means you accept the policy then in force.
