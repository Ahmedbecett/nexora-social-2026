Privacy Policy

Policy last updated: 15 November 2026 · developer: Ahmed Becetti (AI BECETTI).

What we collect

NEXORA AI is an AI workspace for chat, writing, coding, translation, summaries and image generation. This page explains what we collect and why.

  • Account data: name, username, e-mail address or phone number, optional profile photo.
  • AI requests: the messages you send and the outputs you generate, stored in your workspace so you can see your own history.
  • Sign-in: Firebase Authentication (e-mail, Google, phone) or a one-time SMS code.
  • Basic technical data: device/browser type and runtime errors, used only to prevent abuse and fix problems.

How your messages are processed

Your request text is forwarded to one of our AI providers (OpenAI, Google Gemini, OpenRouter, Pollinations, llm7.io) depending on availability, and the answer is returned to your account only. We do not sell your data or use your conversation history from NEXORA servers to target ads. Third-party ad scripts run on web pages; see below for what they can access in your browser.

Ads

The website may show Google AdSense, Monetag and Adsterra ads. Adsterra's Native Banner and Monetag's OnClick/Popunder tag load on public, sign-in and signed-in web pages. Adsterra has its own in-page ad space; Monetag may open another tab or window after a click. Ad networks may process technical data such as IP address, browser identifiers, cookies and page interactions to serve and measure ads. Third-party ad scripts run within the web page and can technically read its content and user inputs. We do not send chat histories from NEXORA servers to ad networks for targeting, but cannot guarantee what third-party code accesses in your browser. Neither Adsterra nor Monetag loads in the native Android app or when the browser signals Global Privacy Control. The Android app shows only Google AdMob ads: a banner on the welcome screen, a rare interstitial (at most once per 10 minutes, 3 per day) at natural transition points such as starting a new chat, and an opt-in rewarded ad the user explicitly requests (grants one day without interstitials only after AdMob confirms the eligible ad was completed). AdMob ads are not shown during text or voice AI conversations or over dialogs. Consent for Android ads is handled by Google's UMP (GDPR) framework where applicable — accept or decline from the in-app consent form. We do not send chat text to AdMob from our servers for ad targeting.

Storage & security

Data is stored on secure infrastructure (Vercel, Neon PostgreSQL, Vercel Blob) over encrypted HTTPS. Provider API keys stay server-side and are never exposed to the client.

Deleting your data

In the app: Account & Security → Danger zone → Delete my account and all my data. Your account and content are permanently erased and cannot be restored. For any data request, e-mail ahmedbecetti35@gmail.com.

Children

The service is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.
