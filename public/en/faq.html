Frequently asked questions

Answers before you get started

Learn what is available in this release and what to expect from an AI assistant. For help with your account, consult the privacy page for contact details.

Using NEXORA AI

NEXORA is a full web platform like Arena AI — no download required. See the feature guide for examples. An optional APK download exists for Android.

What is NEXORA AI?

A full web AI platform — like Arena AI — for chat, writing, coding, translation, summaries and image generation directly in your browser.

Is NEXORA a social networking app?

No. It is a complete AI platform, not a social network with a feed, reels or person-to-person messaging. You can ask it to draft content to publish elsewhere yourself.

Do I need an account?

You can read these pages without an account. To send prompts to the assistant and save conversations, sign in or create an account.

Is it free to use?

The web platform has a free starting tier. Some features may have usage limits, and model availability depends on providers; check the options shown in your account.

Do I need to download an app?

No — NEXORA works fully on the web without installation. An APK is available as an optional download for Android 6.0+.

Are AI answers always accurate?

No. AI can make mistakes. Check important claims and sources before making medical, legal or financial decisions or publishing generated content.
