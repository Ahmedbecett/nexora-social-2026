About the platform

A complete web workspace for ideas

NEXORA AI is a web platform by Ahmed Becetti (AI BECETTI) — inspired by Arena AI — bringing chat, writing, coding and creation together in your browser.

Why a web platform?

Like Arena AI, NEXORA runs fully on the web. No APK is needed — open your browser and start. Chat, writing, coding, translation, summaries and images are available in one workspace so you can move from a first question to a useful draft without switching apps.

An account is needed to send prompts and keep conversation history. The public information pages remain readable without one. An optional Android APK is available for those who prefer an app, but the web platform is complete on its own.

About the name

The project repository and web address include “social”, but this deployed version is NEXORA AI — a web platform like Arena AI. It does not offer a public social feed, reels or direct messaging. Older social screens remain in the repository for reference but are not part of the running platform.

Responsible use

Generated text and images can be useful starting points, not guarantees. Review outputs and verify important claims. Tools such as live search, voice and image generation may vary with device capabilities, usage limits and available providers.
