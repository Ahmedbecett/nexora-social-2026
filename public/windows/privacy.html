NEXORA AI · Windows Edition

Privacy Policy for Windows

This policy covers the official NEXORA AI application for Windows, distributed as an installable PWA and, when published, as an MSIX package in the Microsoft Store.

Version 2.0 · Effective 23 November 2026 · Applies to NEXORA AI 4.5.0 (Windows)

In one paragraph: NEXORA AI uses the same account and production backend as the website and Android app. It stores your session and preferences on your device, sends your prompts over HTTPS to the NEXORA backend and the configured AI providers, and does not sell your data. In the Microsoft Store MSIX app (4.5.0), advertising is provided natively through the official MG Ads (Miracle Games) C# SDK with native CMP consent handling, while website ad scripts are strictly excluded. Settings that require consent stay off until you accept them.

1. Who we are and scope

NEXORA AI is developed and operated by Ahmed Becetti (AI BECETTI), the data controller for the processing described here. The Windows app will be published in the Microsoft Store under the publisher name Ahmedbecetti. The listing is currently a draft.

This policy applies to the NEXORA AI Windows edition: the Progressive Web App installed through Microsoft Edge or Google Chrome, and the MSIX package distributed through the Microsoft Store once certified. It supplements the general NEXORA privacy and cookie policy. Where the two differ for the Windows app, this page applies to the Windows app.

It does not cover third-party websites or services you open from NEXORA (for example GitHub, Vercel, Google or Microsoft), each of which has its own privacy policy.

2. Data we process

Account and identity

  • Name, username, email address or phone number, optional profile image and interface language.
  • Login method (password, Google or phone), account creation date, last sign-in and internal account identifiers.
  • Passwords are stored only as a one-way hash and cannot be recovered by us.

Content you submit

  • Prompts, messages, generated answers and saved conversations.
  • Files you choose to upload (PDF, DOCX, text or images) and the text extracted to answer your request.
  • NEXORA Studio projects, files, terminal commands, checkpoints and deployment links.
  • Preferences such as selected model, answer style, theme and language.

Optional integrations and subscriptions

  • If you connect Google, GitHub or Vercel, we store the access tokens needed for the scopes you approve, encrypted on the server, and only use them for the action you request.
  • Subscription state, plan and payment reference from the payment provider. We do not store full card numbers or banking verification codes.

Technical data

  • IP address, browser/WebView2 version, operating system, pages and times, error messages and request identifiers, used for security, rate limiting and diagnosing failures.
  • We do not collect GPS location, contacts, SMS or call logs. The Windows app does not request camera or microphone access; the web AI screen requests microphone access only when you press the voice button.

3. What is stored on your PC

The Windows app runs in the browser engine (Edge/WebView2) with an origin-scoped storage area. The following keys are stored locally on your device:

Storage keyPurpose
nx_tokenYour current session token, sent only to the NEXORA backend over HTTPS.
nx_accountsUsernames and tokens of accounts you added for quick switching on this device.
nx_meCached basic profile so the app can open faster.
nx_theme, nx_lang, nx_viewTheme, language and last screen preferences.
nx_surfaceMarks that the app was opened from the MSIX package, so Windows-specific rules apply.
nx_ads_consent, nx_ads_frequencyYour advertising choice and a local frequency limit. See the advertising section.

A service worker caches static application files (the interface shell, icons and the offline page) so the app can open without a network. It does not cache API responses, conversation content or requests carrying an authentication header.

You can clear this local data at any time from Windows Settings or the browser’s site-data settings. Clearing it signs you out on that device; server-side data is unaffected.

4. Why we use data

  • To provide the service: authenticate you, keep your conversations and projects, run AI requests and return answers.
  • To operate and secure it: prevent abuse, enforce usage limits, debug failures, protect accounts and comply with legal obligations.
  • To honour your choices: remember language, theme and consent settings and apply them on this device.
  • With consent only: show personalised advertising if a provider is enabled in the future, and only after you accept.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use your conversations or uploaded files to train our own models.

5. Processors and sharing

We share only what is necessary with service providers acting under our instructions or under their own terms:

  • Hosting and delivery: Vercel (application and API hosting).
  • Database: Neon (managed PostgreSQL).
  • AI providers: the configured model providers used to generate answers, images or embeddings for your request.
  • Authentication: Google and Firebase where you choose Google or phone sign-in.
  • Optional integrations: Google Docs/Drive, GitHub and Vercel, only when you connect them and trigger an action.
  • Distribution: Microsoft, for Store installation, updates and any Store-provided diagnostics you enabled in Windows.
  • Advertising: a named ad provider only if advertising is enabled and you consent; see below.

We may also disclose data if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of users and the service. The general privacy policy lists the categories and links in more detail.

6. Advertising

Current status: the Microsoft Store Windows edition (NEXORA AI by BECETTI 4.5.0, native C# + WebView2 MSIX host) integrates the official MG Ads (Miracle Games) C# SDK V1.2.1 for Banner (728×90), optional Rewarded ads, and desktop-guarded Couplet ads (300×600). Interstitial ads remain disabled until an official unit is configured. If an ad fails to load or no inventory is available, no empty advertising space is shown.

Advertising configuration is kept outside application logic in a separate production configuration file and fails closed: if credentials, inventory, or consent are not valid, no ad space is displayed and NEXORA AI continues to work normally.

  • Consent & CMP: MG Ads initializes its native Consent Management Platform (CMP) before requesting ads in applicable regions. Declining personalized ads does not restrict any NEXORA AI features.
  • Real provider measurement only: Impressions, clicks, and rewarded completions are verified and measured exclusively by MG Ads (Miracle Games) under its privacy and advertising policies. The app never generates fake impressions, clicks, or local reward claims.
  • Rewarded ads: Watching a rewarded ad is always 100% optional and user-initiated. Rewards are granted only after MG Ads confirms full completion and fulfillment is reported.
  • Strict separation from website ads: Website-only ad networks (such as Google AdSense, Adsterra, or Monetag) are strictly excluded from the Windows Store MSIX app. Mobile SDKs such as Google AdMob are used on Android only.

You can review or withdraw advertising consent via MG Ads CMP privacy choices in the Windows app or by clearing local app data.

7. Microsoft Store specifics

  • When published, the app will be distributed under the publisher name Ahmedbecetti. The Store listing name is NEXORA AI by BECETTI; the product and website brand remains NEXORA AI.
  • Microsoft processes installation, updates and Store account information under the Microsoft Privacy Statement. We do not receive your Microsoft account password.
  • If the app is offered free of charge, Microsoft does not receive payment data from us. Paid NEXORA subscriptions, if purchased, are handled by the payment provider shown at checkout; they are not processed by the Windows app.
  • The app does not use Windows diagnostic telemetry beyond ordinary web requests to the NEXORA backend. Windows and Edge may collect their own diagnostics according to your Windows settings.
  • Uninstalling the MSIX package removes the package and its app data. A browser-installed PWA is removed from the browser; its site data may remain in your browser profile until you clear site data.

8. Security

  • All communication with the NEXORA backend uses HTTPS. The app refuses to call an insecure API origin.
  • No backend secrets, database credentials or AI provider keys are shipped in the Windows front end. AI requests use the authenticated NEXORA API, never a provider key in the client.
  • Session tokens are stored in the app’s origin-scoped storage and sent only to the NEXORA backend over HTTPS. They are not readable by other websites, but they are not protected by a separate OS credential vault because a PWA/MSIX hosted app has no equivalent of a native credential locker. Anyone who can use your unlocked Windows session and read the app profile may be able to use the session. Protect your Windows account with a password or Windows Hello and sign out on shared devices.
  • You can end all sessions from Account & Security → End sessions on all devices, and delete your account permanently from the danger zone.
  • API responses are validated by the app; errors are shown without exposing internal secrets.

9. Retention and deletion

  • Account, conversations and project data are kept until you delete them or delete your account.
  • Deleting your account removes your account, conversations, generated images and Studio data, and cannot be undone. Backups and security logs may take a short additional period to expire.
  • Local settings and caches remain on your PC until you clear site data or uninstall the app.
  • Payment and tax records may be retained where required by law.

10. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. You can exercise most rights directly in the app, or email ahmedbecetti35@gmail.com. We may need to verify that you control the account. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

11. Children

NEXORA AI is not directed to children under 13 (or under the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child created an account, contact us so we can remove it.

12. AI output and acceptable use

AI answers can be wrong or incomplete. Check important information before relying on it, especially for medical, legal, financial or safety decisions. Do not use the app to violate rights, distribute illegal content or evade security controls. Generated content is your responsibility when you publish it.

13. International transfers

Our providers may process data in countries other than yours. Where required, transfers rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses offered by those providers.

14. Changes

We update this policy when the Windows app or its providers change. The effective date at the top changes with it, and material changes will be announced in the app or on this page. Continuing to use the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.

٣. ما يُخزَّن على جهازك

يعمل تطبيق Windows داخل محرّك المتصفح (Edge/WebView2) بمساحة تخزين مرتبطة بأصل التطبيق. تُخزَّن المفاتيح التالية محليًا على جهازك:

المفتاحالغرض
nx_tokenرمز جلستك الحالية، ويُرسَل إلى خادم NEXORA فقط عبر HTTPS.
nx_accountsأسماء ورموز الحسابات التي أضفتها للتبديل السريع على هذا الجهاز.

تخزّن خدمة العمل (service worker) ملفات الواجهة الثابتة والأيقونات وصفحة عدم الاتصال حتى يفتح التطبيق بلا شبكة. لا تخزّن ردود الـ API ولا المحادثات ولا أي طلب يحمل ترويسة مصادقة.

  • يتحقق التطبيق من ردود الـ API ويعرض الأخطاء دون كشف أسرار داخلية.

٩. الاحتفاظ والحذف

  • تبقى بيانات الحساب والمحادثات والمشاريع حتى تحذفها أو تحذف حسابك.
  • حذف الحساب يمحو حسابك ومحادثاتك والصور المولّدة وبيانات Studio، ولا يمكن التراجع. قد تحتاج النسخ الاحتياطية وسجلات الأمان فترة قصيرة إضافية حتى تنتهي.
  • تبقى الإعدادات والكاش المحلي على جهازك حتى تمسح بيانات الموقع أو تُزيل التطبيق.
  • قد تُحفظ سجلات الدفع والضرائب حيث يوجب القانون ذلك.
